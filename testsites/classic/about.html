About

Malbolge was created by Ben Olmstead in 1998 to be the most difficult programming language possible. Its first program, a Hello, world., took two years and a beam search to find.

MelHttp compiles arbitrary bytes into Malbolge programs and runs them inside a sandboxed, in-process virtual machine, through a CGI-like contract called MelCGI.

Served by melhttpd. Every byte above came out of a Malbolge VM.
