Malbolge transport demo

Once the service worker is active (reload once), every request from this page travels as a Malbolge program. Your browser runs it in the MelHttp VM, compiled to WebAssembly, to get the bytes back.

Open the network tab and look at about.html: the response is Malbolge source text. This is obfuscation, not encryption.
